Privacy Policy

Okurimono Daicho / Last updated: September 8, 2026

In short: this app collects nothing.
Everything you record stays on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else. The app makes no network connections.

Information we collect

None. This app does not do any of the following:

  • Collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information
  • Require an account or sign-in
  • Track usage or include any analytics SDK
  • Show ads or include any advertising SDK
  • Access location, contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Send anything to a server (the app makes no network connections)

Data you enter

The people you add and the gift records you keep (category, date, amount or item, notes, reciprocation due dates and status) are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted, and the developer has no way to view them.

Deleting the app deletes this data from your device. There is no automatic sync between devices.

Export and backup

The app can export your records as CSV, PDF, or a backup file. This happens only when you ask for it, and the file is saved to the location you pick in the Files app. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Exported files contain names and amounts. Please manage where you save or share them.

Notifications

Reminders about reciprocation deadlines are local notifications composed on your device. Their contents are never transmitted.

By default a notification says only that a deadline is due; it does not include the person's name or the gift category, so that details do not appear on your lock screen. You can turn those details on in Settings.

Face ID lock

You can lock the app with Face ID or your device passcode. Biometric data is handled entirely by Apple and is never available to this app. The app only learns whether authentication succeeded.

In-app purchase

Purchases are processed by Apple. Payment details are handled by Apple, and the developer never receives card details or information identifying the purchaser. The app only learns whether the purchase was made.

Sharing with third parties

Since no information is collected, there is nothing to share.

Children's privacy

The app can be used at any age. Because it collects no personal information, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and its last-updated date will be revised. If a future version were to start collecting information, that would be stated in the app update.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact address on the support page.
