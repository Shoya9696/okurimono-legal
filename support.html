Getting started

1. Add a person

On the People tab, tap "+" and enter a name, a reading, and how you know them.

2. Record what you received or gave

On the Records tab, tap "+" and enter the person, category, date, and an amount or item. When you choose "Received", a reciprocation due date is filled in automatically based on the category (49 days for a funeral offering, one month for wedding and birth gifts). You can change the date by hand.

3. Check what you still owe

Anything awaiting reciprocation appears on the first tab, sorted by due date; overdue items are shown in red. A notification arrives at 9:00 a.m. on the due date.

4. Record the return gift

From an event's detail screen you can either mark it as reciprocated, or record the return gift as its own entry.

FAQ

Are the suggested amounts correct?

They are general guidelines only (about half of what you received, or one third to one half for funeral offerings). Appropriate amounts vary by region and by your relationship. Please use your own judgement.

Notifications are not arriving

  • Check Settings → Okurimono Daicho → Notifications is allowed
  • Check that a Focus mode is not suppressing them
  • Check that notifications are on for that specific event, on its detail screen

iOS limits how many pending notifications an app may hold, so the app schedules only the 50 nearest deadlines. Everything else still appears in the list.

What is free?

Up to 5 people and 20 events are free. The reciprocation list and its reminders are always free, no matter how many records you have. A one-time unlock removes the limits and adds CSV/PDF export, Face ID lock, and backup.

I purchased but the app is still locked

Tap "Restore purchase" in Settings. The same Apple Account restores it after reinstalling or changing devices.

Can I sync between devices?

There is no automatic sync. Use the backup export and import (available after unlocking) when changing devices.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. The app makes no network connections. Files are created only when you export, in the location you choose. See the privacy policy.

Does it work on iPad?

This app is for iPhone only.
